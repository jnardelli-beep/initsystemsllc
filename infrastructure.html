Infrastructure

Infrastructure is designed to be minimal, observable, and adaptable. Systems are intentionally small to keep operational complexity under control.

Cloud & Virtual Machines

The environment uses a mix of cloud services and lightweight virtual machines to test deployments, evaluate performance, and support short-lived experiments.

Automation Scripts

Internal scripts orchestrate repeatable tasks such as provisioning, data handling, and lifecycle management. The goal is to keep workflows consistent and auditable.

Monitoring & Orchestration

Monitoring and orchestration are kept high-level with alerting tuned for signal quality rather than noise. Observability is prioritized without exposing sensitive implementation details.
